01Documented print

Phone Stand

Support a phone at a useful viewing angle on a desk.

Why this material
The creator reports eSUN PLA+ for this stand; an impact-modified grade is a reasonable choice for an object that gets handled and knocked.
What it provides
Improved toughness reduces the risk of a brittle break during ordinary bumps.
Tradeoff
PLA+ still creeps under sustained load and should stay away from hot dashboards.

02Application idea

Handheld instrument enclosure prototype

Protect the board and controls during benchtop handling and layout trials.

Why this material
Impact modifiers make the material more forgiving of accidental knocks than a brittle standard PLA shell.
What it provides
Greater impact tolerance gives a handled enclosure prototype more margin against accidental knocks without requiring chamber-dependent printing.
Tradeoff
Toughness does not establish heat resistance, electrical enclosure compliance or drop-test performance.

03Application idea

Low-load robot bumper shell

Provide a replaceable outer cover on a small indoor educational robot.

Why this material
A tough PLA grade can yield under impacts instead of immediately snapping, which suits repeated low-energy collisions.
What it provides
Impact-tolerant walls are less likely to shatter during a low-energy collision, helping protect the prototype behind the bumper.
Tradeoff
The material is less stiff than standard PLA and needs geometry appropriate to the impact.

04Application idea

Portable tool-tray insert

Keep small tools separated inside a carrying case.

Why this material
Impact-modified PLA better tolerates bumps during carrying, while printed pockets locate tools without cutting foam by hand.
What it provides
Tougher pocket edges better tolerate tools bumping against them while the case is moved or unpacked.
Tradeoff
Sustained loads can still deform thin pocket walls, so support heavy tools on the case floor.

05Application idea

Assembly jig with hand-operated stops

Position lightweight parts against stops during repeated manual assembly.

Why this material
PLA+ trades some stiffness for impact tolerance, useful when parts are repeatedly placed against a jig.
What it provides
Stops are less prone to brittle damage from occasional mishandling.
Tradeoff
It is unsuitable for hot assembly operations or highly loaded long-term clamping.
