Read the full material overview

A styrenic rubber rather than a polyurethane: it needs no drying at all, prints at 260–280 °C and 10–30 mm/s, and trades TPU's abrasion resistance for moisture indifference.

Applications

SEBS-based TPE is a styrenic elastomer used for soft-touch surfaces, grips, flexible covers and seals. It provides rubber-like deformation through a different chemistry from TPU. In conventional manufacturing, KRAIBURG documents SEBS-based THERMOLAST W compounds for wet-grip applications that can be processed by multi-component injection molding or extrusion. That is the role of an elastomer surface over a rigid supporting part, not a rigid structural replacement.

Ultrafuse TPS 90A filament has very low moisture uptake and does not require the drying routine typical of TPU, which simplifies printing and storage. Its published abrasion loss is substantially worse than the TPU comparisons, so soft feel should not be confused with a durable wheel tread. Molded grip compounds are formulation-specific: their adhesion or wet-grip performance does not automatically transfer to this filament.

  • Wet-grip elastomer surfaces ↗

    Injection molding and extrusion · KRAIBURG THERMOLAST W, specific SEBS-based molding compounds; family context only · Manufacturer-listed applications

    KRAIBURG describes SEBS-based THERMOLAST W for grip applications, with wet-grip behavior incorporated in the compound. It supports multi-component injection molding and extrusion; these molded-compound properties do not establish the performance of Ultrafuse TPS 90A filament.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
