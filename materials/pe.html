POLYOLEFIN · Material guide

Polyethylene (PE)

Non-polar, chemically inert and effectively non-hygroscopic. One brand — Spectrum's low-warp HDPE — carries the whole retail market, at $87.72/kg; its published 1.1 g/cm3 density and 3.5 GPa modulus put it well above plain polyethylene, so the formulation is clearly modified.
