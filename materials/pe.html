Read the full material overview

Non-polar, chemically inert and effectively non-hygroscopic. One brand — Spectrum's low-warp HDPE — carries the whole retail market, at $87.72/kg; its published 1.1 g/cm3 density and 3.5 GPa modulus put it well above plain polyethylene, so the formulation is clearly modified.

Applications

Polyethylene is used for chemically resistant containers, wear surfaces and packaging, but Spectrum HDPE filament is a modified low-warp formulation rather than ordinary bottle-grade HDPE. Spectrum recommends machine guides exposed to abrasion, housings and technical laboratory components. The useful combination is low moisture uptake, compatibility with many chemicals and resistance to rubbing; those properties matter more than a generic claim that polyethylene is flexible.

The formulation distinction changes the design decision. Spectrum's filament is substantially stiffer and less extensible than ordinary unfilled HDPE stock, so a printed squeeze part will not automatically behave like a molded bottle. Shrinkage still matters for broad flat parts. A container shape or chemical-resistant polymer also does not establish leak-tightness: joints, layer paths and the exact fluid exposure must be considered.

  • Abrasion-exposed machine guides ↗

    FDM · Spectrum HDPE Low Warp filament, modified formulation rather than plain HDPE stock · Manufacturer-listed applications

    Spectrum lists machine components and guides exposed to abrasion among the intended uses of its HDPE filament. Its low-warp formulation combines rigidity with wear resistance; this recommendation does not imply the friction or lifetime of a particular finished guide.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
