Choose a material for a purpose

Five examples and why the material fits

Each example connects a part’s job to the material properties that can help. Application ideas are reasoned suggestions from the linked data. Documented prints link to a creator’s build; the explanation of material choice is our analysis.

01Application idea

PLA console underside support

Print PolySupport for PLA beneath the accessible underside of a PLA console mockup.

Why this material
A matched breakaway interface supports the broad overhang while remaining mechanically removable, so the console body stays PLA and the support is discarded.
What it provides
Mechanical removal avoids a dissolution delay while supporting a cleaner, less drooping PLA overhang during printing.
Tradeoff
This pairing is grade-specific, and removing support from thin PLA edges can still damage them.
Supporting properties and sources
Base polymer / chemistry
Break-away support compounds matched to a specific model material — Polymaker sells separate grades for PLA and for PA12
Typical applications
Support that peels off by hand, avoiding the moisture handling and dissolution time that soluble supports demand
Support material pairing
This is the support material. Polymaker publishes an adhesion matrix per model material, from very good to unusable, because break-away supports only work against the polymer they were matched to

02Application idea

PLA figurine arm support

Print matched PLA breakaway support under an outstretched arm and its fingertips.

Why this material
The separate support material can sustain the overhang without making the model itself from a weak sacrificial polymer; open access lets the support be removed by hand.
What it provides
A separable interface can reduce the force needed to free the arm compared with a fused model-material support.
Tradeoff
Fragile fingers need cautious removal and a tuned interface rather than aggressive pulling.

03Application idea

PA12 bracket counterbore support

Print PolySupport for PA12 inside an accessible downward-facing bolt recess.

Why this material
The PA12-specific support grade is matched to nylon processing and adhesion, allowing the recess roof to print while the bracket remains PA12.
What it provides
The matched removable interface supports the bolt recess while reducing the need to force apart fused PA12 support contacts.
Tradeoff
Confirm the exact PA12 pairing; PLA breakaway support is a different formulation and inaccessible pockets cannot be peeled cleanly.

04Application idea

PA12 duct flange support

Print PA12-matched breakaway support beneath the outer lip of an open duct flange.

Why this material
A removable interface supports the wide lip while leaving the model body in nylon, and the open flange edge provides a route for mechanical removal.
What it provides
A matched peel-away interface can support the flange lip while leaving a practical removal route at its open edge.
Tradeoff
Peel-away support cannot clear a sealed internal passage, and the material-specific adhesion must be tuned.

05Application idea

PLA enclosure latch recess support

Print matched PLA breakaway support below an exposed recess beside an enclosure latch.

Why this material
The support carries the recess roof during printing and then peels away from an opening large enough for access, preserving the PLA latch geometry.
What it provides
A matched removable interface supports the recess roof while avoiding a water-dissolution stage before enclosure assembly.
Tradeoff
Avoid trapping support behind the latch, and test the interface because a brittle model edge can chip during removal.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
