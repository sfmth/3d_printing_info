ABS · Material guide

Flame-retardant ABS (FR-ABS)

ABS with a flame-retardant package rated UL 94 V-0 at 1.5 mm on the base resin; the retardant lifts density to 1.18 g/cm³ and cuts elongation at break from 10 % to 4 %.
