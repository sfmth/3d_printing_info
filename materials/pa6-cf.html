PA · Material guide

Carbon-fibre PA6 (PA6-CF)

The workhorse engineering composite: 102–109 MPa printed XY, HDT to 215 °C at 0.45 MPa and Young's modulus to 8.6 GPa — bought at the price of a 9-hour brass nozzle life and 2.35 % moisture uptake.
