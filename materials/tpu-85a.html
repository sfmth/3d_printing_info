FLEX · Material guide

TPU 85A

Genuinely rubbery TPU at 82–85 Shore A with 650 % elongation and a tensile modulus of 12–22 MPa. Direct drive only, 15–40 mm/s, and the most heat-creep-prone material in the dataset.
