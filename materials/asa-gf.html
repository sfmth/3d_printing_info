Read the full material overview

ASA with 10–15 wt% glass fibre for stiffness and dimensional stability outdoors; ZYLtech requires a hardened nozzle of 0.6 mm or larger and Spectrum recommends ruby or hardened.

Applications

ASA-GF is an outdoor rigidity material for manufacturing tools, housings, covers and exposed mechanical parts. Spectrum lists these uses for ASA-X GF10, connecting glass reinforcement with stiffness and the ASA matrix with resistance to prolonged sunlight and moisture. It fits parts that need to retain a locating or mounting shape rather than act as elastic hinges.

The grade and specimen type matter. Spectrum’s published mechanical and thermal figures include injection-molded specimens, so they are not direct load ratings for a printed bracket. ZYLtech reports limited elongation for its grade, favoring generous roots and distributed loads over narrow flexures. Reinforcement content and required nozzle size differ between manufacturers, even when both products are called ASA-GF.

  • Manufacturing tools and equipment ↗

    FDM · Spectrum ASA-X GF10; 10% glass fibre · Manufacturer-listed applications

    Spectrum lists manufacturing tools and equipment for ASA-X GF10. Stiffness and outdoor stability are the intended advantages; its molded-specimen data do not directly establish the strength of an FDM tool.

  • Housings and covers exposed outdoors ↗

    FDM · Spectrum ASA-X GF10 · Manufacturer-listed applications

    Spectrum lists housings, covers and components requiring high outdoor-exposure resistance. This is a manufacturer-listed use for the exact grade rather than a documented installed enclosure.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
