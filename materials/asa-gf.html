ASA · Material guide

Glass-fibre ASA (ASA-GF)

ASA with 10–15 wt% glass fibre for stiffness and dimensional stability outdoors; ZYLtech requires a hardened nozzle of 0.6 mm or larger and Spectrum recommends ruby or hardened.
