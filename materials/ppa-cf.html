Read the full material overview

Carbon-fibre semi-aromatic nylon: 10–15 wt% chopped fibre in a PPA matrix reaches 188–227 °C HDT at 0.45 MPa and takes up about 1.3 % moisture where the same makers' PA6-CF takes 2.35 %, at the cost of 2–3 % elongation and a 280–320 °C nozzle.
