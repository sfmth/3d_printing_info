FLEX · Material guide

Carbon-fibre TPU

Carbon-filled hard TPU at 70 Shore D for damping mounts and semi-rigid brackets. Extrudr flags it as the one FLEX grade that needs a hardened nozzle, and sells it direct in 0.5 and 2 kg coils; no reseller or second brand was found.
