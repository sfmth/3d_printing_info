HIGHTEMP · Material guide

PEEK

The benchmark high-temperature engineering polymer: 60-100 MPa printed tensile, Tg 143-151 °C, melting 343 °C, UL 94 V-0 and steam-sterilisable. Needs a 380-450 °C hot end and a chamber above 150 °C, and costs $300-770/kg.
