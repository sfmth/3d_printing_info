High-temperature CF nylon (PAHT-CF)

Long-chain carbon-fibre nylon reaching 194 °C HDT at 0.45 MPa and 170 °C at 1.8 MPa, with saturated moisture uptake of 0.88 % — a third of the same maker's PA6-CF.
