01Application idea

PLA articulated mechanism gap support

Print BVOH in reachable gaps around a PLA demonstration linkage.

Why this material
Water-soluble support can be removed from gaps that are awkward to peel, while the linkage remains PLA; Fiberlogy explicitly lists PLA among compatible model materials.
What it provides
Water removal can release supported linkage gaps without levering a rigid support against the mechanism.
Tradeoff
Provide water access and drainage to every supported gap, and keep the highly moisture-sensitive spool dry.

02Application idea

PETG curved internal channel support

Print BVOH beneath the roof of an open-ended PETG demonstration channel.

Why this material
Fiberlogy lists PET-G compatibility, and dissolution can remove support along a curved channel where pliers cannot reach; the channel body remains PETG.
What it provides
Dissolution can clear support from the curved passage where straight mechanical tools cannot reach.
Tradeoff
Water must circulate through the entire channel, and small openings can greatly prolong removal.
Supporting properties and sources
Typical applications
Dissolvable support where PVA dissolves too slowly, and for materials PVA bonds to poorly
Water / humidity resistance in service (1–5)
Dissolves or disintegrates — by design
Soluble in
Water: about 1 g of BVOH per 20 g of water, several hours; warmer water is faster but must not be too hot.

03Application idea

ABS recessed impeller blade support

Print BVOH under the blades of an ABS display impeller.

Why this material
Fiberlogy names ABS compatibility, making water-removable support worth evaluating beneath blades with narrow access; ABS forms the finished object and BVOH is discarded.
What it provides
Water removal can clear blade recesses without forcing each support contact against a thin ABS edge.
Tradeoff
The two-material temperature and adhesion settings need testing, and the soluble filament must stay dry.

04Application idea

ASA duct elbow roof support

Print BVOH below the interior roof of an open-ended ASA duct prototype.

Why this material
Fiberlogy lists ASA compatibility, and dissolvable support offers a route to removing material beyond a straight mechanical reach while leaving the ASA duct intact.
What it provides
Dissolution can clear the curved duct interior while preserving a continuous ASA shell for geometry trials.
Tradeoff
This does not establish airtightness, and the water bath needs access to both ends.

05Application idea

PLA lattice display support

Print BVOH under shallow overhangs inside an open PLA lattice sculpture.

Why this material
Water dissolution makes it possible to remove support from the lattice without snapping each narrow support contact by hand; PLA is an explicitly listed compatible model polymer.
What it provides
Water removal can free many delicate lattice openings without breaking each rigid support contact by hand.
Tradeoff
BVOH is costly and moisture-sensitive, so interface-only use and dry feeding may matter for an economical trial.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
