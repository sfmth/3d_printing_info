PETG · Material guide

Recycled PETG (rPETG)

PETG regranulated from polyester waste. Prusa's own testing shows a 9 % drop in tensile yield and a 17 % drop in interlayer adhesion against virgin Prusament PETG, with diameter tolerance loosening from ±0.02 to ±0.05 mm.
