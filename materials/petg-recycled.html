Read the full material overview

PETG regranulated from polyester waste. Prusa's own testing shows a 9 % drop in tensile yield and a 17 % drop in interlayer adhesion against virgin Prusament PETG, with diameter tolerance loosening from ±0.02 to ±0.05 mm.

Choose a material for a purpose

Five examples and why the material fits

Each example connects a part’s job to the material properties that can help. Application ideas are reasoned suggestions from the linked data. Documented prints link to a creator’s build; the explanation of material choice is our analysis.

01Application idea

Drawer tool organizer

A fitted insert separates hand tools and small workshop accessories.

Why this material
Unfilled recycled PETG retains useful stiffness and PETG-like printing behavior for low-stress organizing tasks. This application does not require assuming identical strength or layer bonding to a virgin grade.
What it provides
Recycled feedstock can serve a useful low-load role without requiring virgin-grade structural performance.
Tradeoff
Recycled grades differ; the catalog also includes a carbon-filled formulation that needs its own process checks.
Supporting properties and sources
Base polymer / chemistry
PETG regranulated from recycled polyester waste; Polymaker's Fiberon PETG-rCF08 adds 8 wt% recycled carbon fibre
Overall printing difficulty (1–5)
Easy — minor profile tuning; Spectrum describes printing as similar to standard PETG
Young's modulus (MPa)
1500 printed horizontal, 1600 printed vertical (Prusament, ISO 527-1) — the same as virgin Prusament PETG
Layer adhesion / Z tensile (MPa)
15 ± 5 (Prusa Polymers interlayer adhesion test), against 18 ± 4 for virgin Prusament PETG

02Application idea

Plant-pot spacing rack

A perforated rack keeps small nursery pots separated on a shelf.

Why this material
Recycled PETG combines moisture resistance with straightforward printing, making it suitable for a lightly loaded organizer exposed to watering. Broad supports keep the task within modest mechanical demands.
What it provides
Moisture resistance makes occasional watering less problematic than it would be for a water-sensitive organizer material.
Tradeoff
PETG can creep over time, so support spans should remain short and loads conservative.
Supporting properties and sources
Enclosure / chamber requirement (1–5)
Open frame is fine — Spectrum states a closed chamber is not necessary
Creep resistance / sustained load (1–5)
Below average — no recycled-grade creep test; virgin PETG sagged from 12 to 17.1–20.4 mm over 5 days at 1.25 kg
Chemical resistance
Withstands acids, alkalis, oils and UV exposure

03Application idea

Cable-management comb

A screwed comb routes low-voltage cables neatly along a desk edge.

Why this material
An unfilled recycled PETG grade offers enough stiffness for static cable organization while tolerating ordinary handling. The role avoids highly strained snap features, where reduced interlayer strength relative to virgin material could matter more.
What it provides
PETG-like stiffness keeps cables separated while recycled feedstock serves a mechanically undemanding role.
Tradeoff
Check filament diameter and the actual grade before relying on thin interference-fit details.

04Application idea

Vacuum accessory storage dock

A wall-mounted dock holds idle vacuum nozzles between uses.

Why this material
Recycled PETG is a reasonable candidate for a lightly loaded storage part because its printing behavior and heat deflection can resemble the corresponding virgin grade. Broad cradles avoid concentrating force in small hooks.
What it provides
Retained heat deflection makes ordinary indoor accessory storage plausible without needing virgin-grade strength assumptions.
Tradeoff
Layer adhesion may be lower than virgin PETG, so orient mounting features and allow generous material around screws.
Supporting properties and sources
Overall printing difficulty (1–5)
Easy — minor profile tuning; Spectrum describes printing as similar to standard PETG
Layer adhesion / Z tensile (MPa)
15 ± 5 (Prusa Polymers interlayer adhesion test), against 18 ± 4 for virgin Prusament PETG
HDT at 0.45 MPa (°C)
68 (ISO 75, 0.45 MPa) — the same as virgin Prusament PETG; Spectrum quotes 70

05Application idea

Workshop parts sorting tray

A compartmented tray separates fasteners during disassembly and repair.

Why this material
Recycled PETG provides useful rigidity and resistance to incidental oil exposure for an organizer that sees little structural loading. Choosing an unfilled grade keeps this distinct from the recycled-carbon-fibre product also listed in the category.
What it provides
Oil resistance helps the tray stay useful through routine disassembly work with greasy fasteners.
Tradeoff
Unknown solvent exposure and grade variability still matter; test cleaners before soaking the tray.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
