PA · Material guide

Carbon-fibre PA12 (PA12-CF)

Carbon-fibre nylon on a PA12 matrix, chosen for humidity stability rather than peak strength: 1.5 % equilibrium moisture against 3.33 % for PA6-GF25, and the only nylon in Polymaker's conditioning test that did not grow past its original size.
