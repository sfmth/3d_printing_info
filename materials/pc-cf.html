PC · Material guide

Carbon-fibre PC (PC-CF)

PC with chopped carbon fibre: flexural modulus rises from about 2.1 to 3.5–6.5 GPa and HDT at 1.8 MPa from 93 to 106 °C, and unusually the fibre raises interlayer adhesion rather than cutting it.
