HIGHTEMP · Material guide

PPS

Semi-crystalline, melting at 280-283 °C, UL 94 V-0 and resistant to essentially every solvent below 200 °C. Unusually ductile for this class at 18 % printed elongation, and the one high-temperature polymer both its suppliers rate explicitly for creep.
