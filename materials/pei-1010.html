PEI (ULTEM 1010)

The highest heat deflection in the dataset — 215 °C at 1.8 MPa on printed Stratasys coupons — and the most demanding process: a 410 °C nozzle, a 90 °C chamber and a 150 °C drying step. Z strength is the weak point, at 36-40 % of in-plane on Stratasys hardware.
