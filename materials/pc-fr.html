Read the full material overview

UL 94 V-0 polycarbonate for electrical enclosures; the retardant roughly halves elongation at break to 2.4–3.5 % and, in Bambu Lab's grade, cuts saturated water absorption to 0.12 %.

Applications

Flame-retardant PC is important in electrical and transportation components where a useful engineering plastic must also meet specified material flammability requirements. Its value is the combination of a rigid, heat-resistant enclosure and a documented FR formulation, rather than flame retardancy alone. Polymaker reports that CURE Mannheim uses PolyMax PC-FR for TS housings and PCB enclosures in its Formula Student vehicle, selecting it for thermal stability and electrical applications.

The flame-retardant formulation has limited elongation, so thin latches and sharply loaded mounting bosses can be less forgiving than the housing walls. Drying and layer bonding remain important to mechanical reliability. Flammability classification depends on the specific grade, thickness and test conditions, while a heat-deflection result describes a loaded test specimen rather than a universal continuous operating temperature.

  • CURE Mannheim electronics enclosures ↗

    FDM/FFF printing · PolyMax PC-FR in the documented CURE Mannheim application; not generic FR PC certification · Documented case

    Polymaker reports PolyMax PC-FR use in the Formula Student team vehicle for TS housings and PCB enclosures, citing thermal stability, layer adhesion and its flame-retardant formulation. The case does not transfer vehicle safety approval to other printed enclosures.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
