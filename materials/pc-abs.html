Read the full material overview

A PC/ABS alloy that keeps most of PC's heat resistance while printing closer to ABS; notched Charpy of 25.8 kJ/m² beats both parents, and it still holds 13 kJ/m² at −30 °C.

Applications

PC/ABS balances toughness and heat resistance with the processing behavior and finish of an ABS-containing blend. This explains its position in automotive trim and electronics packaging, where a part must hold mounting features and survive handling. Webasto and Covestro demonstrate the combination in a two-shot roof sensor module bezel: a transparent PC first shot provides sensor windows, while mineral-reinforced PC/ABS forms the opaque supporting structure.

The ABS phase improves processing balance, but the blend remains sensitive to UV exposure and several solvents. FDM parts still need controlled moisture and adequate layer bonding, particularly around bearing seats and screw bosses. Notched-impact performance varies with temperature and formulation; a mineral-filled molding grade prioritizing dimensional stability will not behave like an unfilled, highly ductile blend.

  • Webasto roof sensor module bezel ↗

    Two-shot injection molding · Mineral-reinforced Bayblend T95 MF PC/ABS plus a separate transparent PC grade; not unfilled PC/ABS filament · Documented case

    Covestro documents an injection-molded prototype bezel with a transparent Makrolon AG first shot and a mineral-reinforced Bayblend T95 MF PC/ABS second shot. Combining sensor transparency and a supporting opaque structure serves the module design.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
