HIGHTEMP · Material guide

PEKK

Amorphous PEKK avoids PEEK's crystallisation-control problem and prints at a lower chamber temperature. Unfilled grades give 73-90 MPa printed in plane; the 15 % carbon aerospace grade posts the highest printed tensile, flexural and modulus figures in this dataset at 126, 178 and 9125 MPa.
