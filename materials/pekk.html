Read the full material overview

Amorphous PEKK avoids PEEK's crystallisation-control problem and prints at a lower chamber temperature. Unfilled grades give 73-90 MPa printed in plane; the 15 % carbon aerospace grade posts the highest printed tensile, flexural and modulus figures in this dataset at 126, 178 and 9125 MPa.

Choose a material for a purpose

Five examples and why the material fits

Each example connects a part’s job to the material properties that can help. Application ideas are reasoned suggestions from the linked data. Documented prints link to a creator’s build; the explanation of material choice is our analysis.

01Application idea

Complex heated-fixture bracket

Print an unfilled amorphous PEKK bracket for a noncritical thermal fixture.

Why this material
Amorphous PEKK avoids PEEK's crystallization-control problem, making it attractive when a complex shape needs the PAEK family's properties but process control is a major concern.
What it provides
Reduced crystallization-control demands can make a complex PAEK fixture more manageable to produce consistently on suitable equipment.
Tradeoff
It still needs industrial high-temperature printing, and carbon-filled PEKK figures do not describe the unfilled part.
Supporting properties and sources
Base polymer / chemistry
Polyether ketone ketone, amorphous grade — a polyaryletherketone that stays amorphous, so it does not need PEEK's crystallisation control. Stratasys sells it as Antero 800NA and Antero 840CN03; category numbers below are unfilled unless a carbon grade is named
Enclosure / chamber requirement (1–5)
Actively heated chamber required
Tensile strength (XY) (MPa)
86.7-90.0 at yield and 73.0 at break (printed XZ, ASTM D638, Antero 800NA on the F900 and Fortus 450mc); 88.8 at break annealed; 126 at break for CarbonX PEKK-A CF15 (printed XY, ISO 527)

02Application idea

Solvent-cleaned assembly nest

Print an unfilled PEKK nest that holds a component during compatible solvent cleaning.

Why this material
The catalog's chemical-compatibility information makes PEKK relevant where common organic cleaning solvents attack other plastics; a rigid nest can maintain repeatable component spacing.
What it provides
Resistance to compatible organic solvents can preserve locating surfaces through repeated assembly-cleaning cycles.
Tradeoff
Confirm the exact chemical and printed integrity, especially with concentrated acids; the nest is not a sealed chemical vessel.
Supporting properties and sources
Soluble in
Concentrated sulphuric acid, as for PEEK; no common organic solvent touches a polyaryletherketone.
Softened or crazed by
Concentrated sulphuric acid and liquid Freon are the entries Ensinger marks not resistant; acetone, MEK and toluene do nothing.
Elasticity / flexibility (1–5)
Rigid

03Application idea

Low-voltage insulating fixture spacer

Print an unfilled PEKK spacer for a noncritical low-voltage benchtop fixture.

Why this material
The catalog identifies unfilled PEKK as electrically insulating, so it is a candidate when separation and a rigid complex shape are both required.
What it provides
The unfilled insulating grade can separate conductive fixture components without adding a deliberately dissipative material path.
Tradeoff
Carbon-filled and ESD PEKK grades behave differently, and insulation of a printed assembly needs appropriate testing.
Supporting properties and sources
Electrical & ESD properties
Unfilled PEKK is an insulator at above 1.4e14 ohm-cm (ASTM D257); CarbonX PEKK-A CF15 sits above 10^7 ohm/sq, and the Antero 840CN03 ESD grade is specified at 10^4-10^9 ohm-cm
Base polymer / chemistry
Polyether ketone ketone, amorphous grade — a polyaryletherketone that stays amorphous, so it does not need PEEK's crystallisation control. Stratasys sells it as Antero 800NA and Antero 840CN03; category numbers below are unfilled unless a carbon grade is named

04Application idea

Stiff gripper mounting plate

Print a carbon-filled PEKK plate for an experimental robot gripper.

Why this material
The named CF15 grade has much higher printed modulus than unfilled PEKK, making it relevant when a mounting plate should resist bending under modest loads.
What it provides
High CF-grade stiffness can help the tested gripper preserve alignment under bending loads.
Tradeoff
The carbon-filled grade has low elongation and requires abrasion-resistant printing hardware; its numbers cannot be assigned to unfilled PEKK.
Supporting properties and sources
Young's modulus (MPa)
2640-3870 (printed XZ, ASTM D638, Antero 800NA; upper figure annealed); 9125 for CarbonX PEKK-A CF15 (printed XY, ISO 527)
Elongation at break (%)
6.1 at break (printed XZ, ASTM D638, Antero 800NA); 2.3 in ZX; 3.15 for CarbonX PEKK-A CF15 (ISO 527)
Abrasiveness (1–5)
Highly abrasive — most PEKK filament sold is carbon-filled; unfilled Antero 800NA and ThermaX PEKK-A are non-abrasive

05Application idea

Temperature-cycling alignment comb

Print an unfilled PEKK comb that positions several test coupons.

Why this material
Published printed expansion and elevated-temperature strength-retention data provide a basis for evaluating PEKK when coupon spacing must remain predictable during a controlled thermal experiment.
What it provides
Known printed expansion behavior helps plan clearances so coupon positioning remains useful during the tested thermal cycle.
Tradeoff
Expansion differs by print direction, and the fixture's allowable temperature and load require direct validation.
Supporting properties and sources
Thermal expansion (CTE) (µm/m·K)
36.1 in XY and 50.2 in ZX (printed, 40-140 °C, ASTM E831, Antero 800NA); 47.4 and 51.2 for the ESD grade Antero 840CN03
Creep resistance / sustained load (1–5)
Excellent — holds load for years. Antero 800NA still holds 82 % of its room-temperature ZX tensile strength at 104 °C and 63 % at 132 °C (printed, ASTM D638)
Elasticity / flexibility (1–5)
Rigid

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
