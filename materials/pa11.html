PA11 (Nylon 11)

Bio-based nylon from castor oil, sitting between PA12 and PA6 for moisture. Prusa's grade posts an interlayer adhesion of 32 MPa and a Z-to-XY tensile ratio of 0.84 — the most isotropic filament in their range.
