Tradeoff

02Application idea

Bedside remote locator tile

Attach a glowing decorative marker to a remote-control storage spot.

Why this material
A phosphorescent grade can emit visible light after charging, making it useful for identifying an object without powered illumination.
What it provides
The marker needs no battery or wiring.
Tradeoff
Brightness fades after exposure and depends on geometry, so test visibility in the intended room.

03Application idea

Indoor board-game counters

Distinguish game pieces during a dim-light themed game.

Why this material
The glow additive supplies the visual effect throughout the printed material, rather than relying on a thin painted surface.
What it provides
The material’s afterglow can distinguish pieces in dim conditions without embedding a separate light source in every counter.
Tradeoff
The brittle material needs robust edges and should not be treated as a durable outdoor marker.

04Application idea

Cosplay luminous rune insert

Add a glowing ornamental feature to a costume prop.

Why this material
A thick inset can make use of the glow grade’s thickness-dependent brightness while the surrounding prop supplies support.
What it provides
The visual effect can be incorporated without electronics inside the prop.
Tradeoff
Fine features and tiny nozzles are a poor match, and the abrasive additive requires a hardened nozzle.

05Application idea

Non-safety indoor direction plaque

Provide a novelty arrow or room label that remains visible after lights switch off.

Why this material
Phosphorescent PLA is suited to decorative signage where afterglow is useful and no regulated performance is required.
What it provides
The light-charged material can keep a decorative label visible briefly after lights switch off without powering the plaque.
Tradeoff
Afterglow is not dependable emergency illumination, and visibility must be checked after realistic charging.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
