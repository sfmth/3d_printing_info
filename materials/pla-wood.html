PLA · Material guide

Wood-filled PLA

PLA carrying 10–18 wt% wood powder: it sands and stains like timber, absorbs 1.25 % water — the most of any PLA grade — and loses about a third of plain PLA's tensile strength.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
