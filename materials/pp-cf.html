Carbon-fibre PP (PP-CF)

Carbon-fibre polypropylene: HDT jumps to 158 °C at 0.45 MPa while density stays at 0.91 g/cm³, the best stiffness-to-weight in the dataset. Prusa states a hardened nozzle is necessary.

Carbon-fibre PP (PP-CF) — Solvents & Solubility
Property and unitPublished value and conditionsSources and dates
Soluble inNone at room temperature; chloroform dissolves the matrix at 60 °C and xylene at about 118 °C, leaving the fibre.
  • Pro-fax and Moplen polypropylene — Chemical Resistance Guide (20, 60 and 100 °C) · 2026-08 · tds
