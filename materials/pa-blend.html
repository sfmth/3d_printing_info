PA · Material guide

PA6/66 copolymer blends

A PA6/66 copolymer that prints more easily than straight PA6. Polymaker publishes full dry and wet printed blocks for it: at 6.16 % moisture, tensile falls 78 to 34 MPa and modulus 2703 to 724 MPa, while notched impact quadruples.
