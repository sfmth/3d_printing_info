PLA · Material guide

Lightweight / foaming PLA (LW-PLA)

PLA with a blowing agent. Active-foaming grades expand at the nozzle and reach print densities near 0.40 g/cm³ at reduced flow; pre-foamed grades ship at 0.8–0.9 g/cm³ and do not expand further.

Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change the result. Unknown values are —; a listed use is not a certification.
