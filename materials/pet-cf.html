PETG · Material guide

Carbon-fibre PET (PET-CF)

Semi-crystalline PET with carbon fibre — a genuinely different material from PETG-CF, reaching 182–205 °C heat deflection but needing an 80 °C dry, a heated chamber and speeds halved to 100 mm/s.
