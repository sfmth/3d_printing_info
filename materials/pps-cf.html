Fibre-reinforced PPS

Filled PPS reaches 252 °C heat deflection at 0.45 MPa and Polymaker states its grade needs no heated chamber — the one way into this temperature class without industrial hardware. Elongation at break is 1.4 %, the lowest in the dataset.
