Read the full material overview

Semi-aromatic nylon: aromatic diacid units lift the melting point to about 231 °C and the whole family is sold on lower moisture uptake than PA6, but the unfilled grade still deflects near its 80 °C Tg — the 190–227 °C heat-deflection figures quoted for PPA belong to the carbon-filled grades.

Applications

PPA is a broad semi-aromatic nylon family important where dimensional stability, chemical resistance and temperature performance exceed the needs of ordinary nylon applications. Its industrial role includes connectors, pumps and other tightly fitted parts, but those uses usually involve carefully selected compounds. BASF documents a concrete example: Weidmüller uses Ultramid Advanced N3U41 G6 PA9T for OMNIMATE 4.0 PCB connectors and terminals, valuing processing, dimensional stability and compatibility with reflow assembly.

Unfilled PPA makes a different compromise from reinforced connector compounds: its low-friction behavior suits sliding parts, but loaded heat resistance falls near its glass transition and declines further after moisture saturation. The high melting point therefore says little about a loaded bracket. Dry processing, grade selection and conditioned fit remain important, and annealing can introduce warping rather than automatically improve dimensions.

  • Weidmüller OMNIMATE 4.0 PCB connectors ↗

    Injection molding · Specialized Ultramid Advanced N3U41 G6 PA9T formulation; not the unfilled Siraya PPA filament · Documented case

    BASF reports Weidmüller use of Ultramid Advanced N3U41 G6 in connectors and terminals. Dimensional stability, chemical resistance, color coding and temperature performance support compact components and reflow assembly. These are application-specific molding-material capabilities.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
