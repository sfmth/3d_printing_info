Read the full material overview

Semi-aromatic nylon: aromatic diacid units lift the melting point to about 231 °C and the whole family is sold on lower moisture uptake than PA6, but the unfilled grade still deflects near its 80 °C Tg — the 190–227 °C heat-deflection figures quoted for PPA belong to the carbon-filled grades.

Choose a material for a purpose

Five examples and why the material fits

Each example connects a part’s job to the material properties that can help. Application ideas are reasoned suggestions from the linked data. Documented prints link to a creator’s build; the explanation of material choice is our analysis.

01Application idea

Application idea: small indexing gear

Print a low-speed gear for a light-duty indexing demonstration.

Why this material
The unfilled PPA supplier describes the grade as low-friction and intended for gears, while its stiffness makes it a candidate for maintaining tooth alignment.
What it provides
A qualified low-friction, stiff tooth surface could preserve alignment while the gear transmits modest motion.
Tradeoff
Test wear and conditioned fit; unfilled PPA thermal limits are much lower than impressive figures published for carbon-filled PPA grades.
Supporting properties and sources
Wear resistance & friction
Siraya Tech describes the unfilled grade as low-friction and self-lubricating, aimed at gears and bearings
Flexural modulus (MPa)
3350 (printed, ISO 178, Siraya Tech)
HDT at 1.8 MPa (°C)
73.5 dry and 48.5 saturated (printed, ISO 75 Method A at 1.8 MPa, Siraya Tech) — unfilled PPA deflects near its Tg; the 190–227 °C figures quoted for PPA are carbon-filled grades
Effect of moisture on strength
Siraya Tech publishes dry against saturated for the same grade: tensile 70 → 60 MPa, flexural 114 → 87 MPa, HDT at 1.8 MPa 73.5 → 48.5 °C, while Charpy rises 10 → 14.5 kJ/m²

02Application idea

Application idea: sliding carriage guide

Print a replaceable guide shoe for a hand-positioned instrument carriage.

Why this material
The unfilled grade low-friction description provides a reason to investigate sliding contact, while rigidity helps the shoe locate against the rail.
What it provides
A qualified low-friction guide surface can permit smooth positioning while a rigid body locates the carriage.
Tradeoff
Validate surface finish, wear and dimensional fit after humidity exposure because saturation lowers strength and heat-deflection resistance.

03Application idea

Application idea: oily sensor mounting saddle

Print a saddle that holds a lightweight sensor near an oil-lubricated machine.

Why this material
PPA chemical resistance makes it a candidate for incidental oil exposure, and unfilled-grade stiffness can help maintain the sensor position.
What it provides
A chemically compatible, rigid saddle can retain sensor position after incidental exposure to the validated oil.
Tradeoff
Check the exact fluid and working temperature; moisture still alters the grade, and chemical-family descriptions do not qualify every industrial solvent.
Supporting properties and sources
Chemical resistance
Withstands fuels, oils and industrial chemicals; Bambu Lab rates semi-aromatic nylon better than general nylon against weak acids, weak alkalis and most organic solvents
Elasticity / flexibility (1–5)
Rigid — 3.6 GPa tensile modulus
Effect of moisture on strength
Siraya Tech publishes dry against saturated for the same grade: tensile 70 → 60 MPa, flexural 114 → 87 MPa, HDT at 1.8 MPa 73.5 → 48.5 °C, while Charpy rises 10 → 14.5 kJ/m²
Softened or crazed by
Mineral acids, oxidants, halogens and zinc chloride solutions attack the amide bond; ordinary solvents do not.

04Application idea

Application idea: precision assembly locator

Print a small locating nest for repeated manual assembly at room temperature.

Why this material
Unfilled PPA stiffness supports positioning surfaces, while its lack of fibre avoids needing an abrasive composite solely to obtain a rigid engineering part.
What it provides
Stiff unfilled locating surfaces can resist assembly deflection while avoiding fibre-filled filament abrasion.
Tradeoff
Verify repeatability after conditioning and do not anneal by assumption: supplier guidance conflicts and warns that prints can warp.
Supporting properties and sources
Flexural modulus (MPa)
3350 (printed, ISO 178, Siraya Tech)
Abrasiveness (1–5)
Non-abrasive when unfilled
Effect of moisture on strength
Siraya Tech publishes dry against saturated for the same grade: tensile 70 → 60 MPa, flexural 114 → 87 MPa, HDT at 1.8 MPa 73.5 → 48.5 °C, while Charpy rises 10 → 14.5 kJ/m²
Annealing response
Siraya Tech's product page offers annealing for strength while its own data sheet says annealing is not recommended because the print can warp; on its 15 % carbon-filled PPA, annealing lifts HDT at 1.8 MPa from 82.5 to 119 °C

05Application idea

Application idea: low-speed cam follower pad

Print a replaceable pad that slides against a slowly operated demonstration cam.

Why this material
The supplier low-friction description motivates evaluating sliding contact, and grade rigidity can help hold the follower geometry under modest forces.
What it provides
A qualified low-friction pad can provide the sliding interface while rigid geometry maintains cam contact.
Tradeoff
Measure wear, temperature and creep in the actual assembly; unfilled PPA is moisture-sensitive and does not inherit carbon-filled PPA heat resistance.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
