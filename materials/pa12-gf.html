PA · Material guide

Glass-fibre PA12 (PA12-GF)

Glass-fibre PA12: 7.8 GPa tensile modulus with 4.5 % elongation at break, nearly double the ductility of glass-filled PA6, and far less humidity drift because of the PA12 matrix.
