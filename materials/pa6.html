Read the full material overview

The toughest and most abrasion-resistant common nylon, and the most moisture-hungry: it must be printed dry, in an enclosure, and a conditioned part is measurably softer and larger than the same part dry.

Applications

PA6 is a workhorse nylon for wear parts and mechanically durable components. Toughness and abrasion resistance explain its use in gears, bushings and sliding guides, where a contact surface must survive repeated rubbing rather than simply carry a one-time tensile load. It also serves larger injection-molded parts: BASF documents Ultramid B3K PA6 in BOOXit reusable transport boxes, selected for robust handling and efficient molding.

Water uptake is the central compromise: conditioned PA6 becomes softer and changes dimensions, directly affecting gear backlash and bushing clearance. Increased impact toughness after conditioning can therefore come at the expense of positional accuracy. FDM layer direction and surface finish also matter, while actual sliding wear depends on the mating material, contact pressure, speed and lubrication.

  • BOOXit reusable transport boxes ↗

    Injection molding · Ultramid B3K BK00464 PA6 molding resin; not a filled nylon or FDM filament result · Documented case

    BASF reports that BOOXit uses Ultramid B3K BK00464 PA6 for reusable transport boxes, connecting durability and impact resistance with an easy-flow, fast-processing molding grade. The documented benefit concerns repeated logistics handling and economical large-component manufacture.

How to read these values

Values describe representative grades, not every spool. Manufacturer datasheets and independent tests retain their source labels and dates. Printed orientation, test method, conditioning and formulation can change results. Unknown values are —. A listed use is not a certification. Shore A, Shore D and Rockwell hardness are separate scales.
