Privacy

Updated 29 October 2026. This notice describes the 3D Printing Info website published from sfmth/3d_printing_info.

Hosting and ordinary requests

This is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. Your browser sends GitHub the information needed to request pages and local assets, including your IP address. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not visitors are signed in. These hosting requests happen independently of your analytics choice. Read GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Optional Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, measurement ID G-12FDG0Z8JB, to understand visits and improve this reference. It is off until you select Allow analytics. Before consent, or after you decline, this site does not load Google's analytics script, configure Analytics, or send analytics or consent pings to Google.

If you allow analytics, your browser contacts Google to load its code and send usage information. This can include page visits and interactions, referrer information, browser/device information and cookie identifiers; Google receives the network information needed for those requests. Our page-location configuration excludes this site's query strings and fragments. Analytics cookies are scoped to this site's path, use the 3dpi prefix and are configured to expire after up to one year. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled by this site's configuration; advertising consent remains denied.

Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. Google's processing and storage also depend on its service and the Analytics account settings; this page does not promise a particular server-side retention period.

Your choice and withdrawal

Analytics settings in every footer lets you allow or decline analytics at any time. Declining after a grant withdraws consent, disables Analytics collection, attempts to remove this site's accessible analytics cookies, and reloads the page to unload Google's running code. It cannot undo requests already sent or delete data already held by Google. Browser controls can also remove cookies and site data.

Your choice is stored in this browser's local storage under 3dpi.analyticsConsent.v1 until you change it or clear site data. It is not sent to our own server. Another browser or device has a separate choice. If storage is blocked, the choice applies to the current page and may be requested again on later pages. Without JavaScript, analytics stays off and the settings control is unavailable.

Theme, filters and external links

The comparison theme follows your browser's color preference unless you change it on that page. That selection is kept in page memory, not a theme cookie or local-storage preference. Comparison controls process catalog data in your browser; some selections can appear in the page URL.

Links to manufacturers, source documents, creator platforms and other services take you to third-party sites with their own privacy practices. Following a link sends a request to that site and may include referrer information according to your browser's policy. Declining analytics here does not control those other sites.

Questions

You can contact the site maintainer through the repository issue tracker. Issues are public and GitHub's privacy terms apply; avoid posting personal or sensitive information.
