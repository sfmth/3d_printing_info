Creep and stress relaxation in FDM parts

Use measured creep and retained-stress evidence to screen continuously loaded brackets, locating fixtures and fastened joints, with time, environment and control mode kept attached.

3D Printing Info · Published 2026-10-18 · Revised 2026-10-18

Editorial synthesis assisted by AI, based on the linked experimental research. No mechanical testing was performed for this article.

Decide whether position or retained force must stay stable

Engineering interpretation. A support carrying a steady force can gradually deflect: that is the creep question. A clip or interference fit held at a set deformation can lose force: that is the stress-relaxation question. Specify which change would make the part fail its function, then seek a test with the same loading condition. Fischbach and Weinberg [details] · Liber-Kneć and Łagan / Materials [details]

Engineering interpretation. The printed PLA experiments below demonstrate both responses near room temperature. They use different grades, specimen shapes, load controls and durations, so the results remain separate. For a fixture, use them to choose a relevant hold test and acceptance limit; they do not choose a universally creep-resistant polymer. Fischbach and Weinberg [details] · Liber-Kneć and Łagan / Materials [details]

Constant force and fixed deformation produce different evidence

In a constant-load creep test, record deformation over time. Compliance expresses strain per unit stress; creep modulus expresses the corresponding stress-to-strain ratio at a stated time. A lower creep modulus during the hold means greater deformation under that load. It is different from the initial tensile modulus in a quick pull test. Fischbach and Weinberg [details]

Engineering interpretation. In a fixed-strain relaxation test, hold deformation and record how much stress remains. For a spring clip, retained force may matter more than its initial stiffness. A bracket under a hanging weight calls for displacement measurements instead. A bolted joint combines the stiffness of the fastener, washers, contacts and printed member, so its behavior needs an assembly test. Liber-Kneć and Łagan / Materials [details] · Amza et al. / Polymers [details]

For a loaded fixture, measure drift at the working surface

Fischbach and Weinberg’s Ultrafuse PLA beam continued to creep through the end of its week-long bending hold, even though the test was well below the grade’s reported glass transition. The final creep modulus came from one specimen. Its unknown raster-to-load-axis definition and crosshead-based strain measurement matter when deciding how closely the experiment represents a proposed part. Fischbach and Weinberg [details] · Fischbach and Weinberg [details]

Engineering interpretation. For a locating fixture or overhung bracket, define the allowed movement where the part locates or supports the work. Test its actual section, lever arm and build orientation under a representative sustained load. A room-temperature coupon endpoint cannot determine the drift of a warmer bracket, or establish a general reduction factor for its static strength. Fischbach and Weinberg [details]

For a constrained feature, measure force after the hold

The ROSA PLA Starter result shows that holding deformation does not hold stress constant. Its maximum and one-hour residual values describe the same 2% strain protocol. The paper’s infinity subscript labels a one-hour endpoint; it does not demonstrate that relaxation has ended for all later times. Liber-Kneć and Łagan / Materials [details] · Liber-Kneć and Łagan / Materials [details]

Engineering interpretation. For a clip, spring feature or interference fit, set the installed deformation and measure the remaining force through the required holding period and environmental range. The 2% tensile strain in this experiment is a test condition, not an allowable strain for a differently shaped clip. Include installation damage and repeat assembly if the part is reused. Liber-Kneć and Łagan / Materials [details]

Removal torque is useful joint evidence with a limited meaning

Amza and colleagues tested washer-loaded M6 assemblies in printed Fillamentum PLA Extrafill and PrimaSelect PETG. After seven days, the screws required less torque to remove than the original tightening torque. That observation can motivate a joint-retention test, but the experiment did not directly measure bolt preload or a fixed-strain retained stress. Amza et al. / Polymers [details]

Engineering interpretation. For a clamped fixture, specify the minimum retained clamping force or the permitted slip, then measure that quantity on the assembled joint. Record tightening procedure, washer geometry, contact area and operating cycles. A metal compression limiter or a spring element is a candidate design change to evaluate in that test; a removal-torque percentage alone cannot qualify it. Amza et al. / Polymers [details]

Keep specimen age, moisture and measurement method attached

The creep beam was loaded five hours after printing. Its laboratory humidity varied over the reported range, and the authors calculated strain from crosshead travel. The relaxation study reports five specimens per case and standard deviation, but does not specify prior moisture equilibration, time since printing or the relaxation strain ramp rate. Expand the records for the exact printing and test details. Fischbach and Weinberg [details] · Liber-Kneć and Łagan / Materials [details]

Engineering interpretation. These studies do not establish a common-condition comparison for PETG, ASA, PC, nylon or their fiber composites. A supplier’s molded-compound creep curve also needs its grade, process, loading direction and environment checked before it can inform a printed component. Request printed-grade evidence or conduct a representative test when those conditions are missing. Fischbach and Weinberg [details] · Liber-Kneć and Łagan / Materials [details]

A hold test that resolves the design decision

Engineering interpretation. Select the observable first: displacement for a loaded support, retained force for a constrained feature, or clamping force and slip for an assembled joint. Set the acceptable limit and holding period from the component’s function. Fischbach and Weinberg [details] · Liber-Kneć and Łagan / Materials [details] · Amza et al. / Polymers [details]

  1. Engineering interpretation. Print repeated specimens or components in the intended grade, build orientation, wall/infill layout and thermal history. Record time since printing and moisture conditioning. Include the service temperature and humidity range rather than relying on a Tg or HDT entry. Fischbach and Weinberg [details] · Liber-Kneć and Łagan / Materials [details]

  2. Engineering interpretation. Log force and displacement from loading through the hold. Verify that the intended force or strain remains controlled. After unloading, measure recovery and any residual set that affects fit or reuse. For a joint, retain the actual fastener and contact stack. Fischbach and Weinberg [details] · Amza et al. / Polymers [details]

  3. Engineering interpretation. If the requirement extends beyond the measured duration, obtain longer tests or validate an acceleration/modeling method against independent holds. Check whether deformation, force loss or failure mode changes with load and environment. The published one-hour and week-long results cannot establish years of service. Fischbach and Weinberg [details] · Liber-Kneć and Łagan / Materials [details]

    Fischbach and Weinberg · Independent experiment · document date: 2023-08-16 · version: arXiv:2302.11240v2 · accessed 2026-10-18
