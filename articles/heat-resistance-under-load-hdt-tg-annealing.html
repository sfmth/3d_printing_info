Heat resistance under load: HDT, Tg, annealing and service temperature

Read thermal measurements by their method, stress and material state before selecting a hot fixture, housing or tool. An HT-PLA example shows why annealing and load belong beside the temperature.

Editorial synthesis assisted by AI, based on the linked technical documents and experimental research. No thermal or mechanical testing was performed for this article.

A thermal number needs a load, a duration and a process

Engineering interpretation. Start with the temperature at the loaded feature and the permitted dimensional drift. HDT is useful screening evidence when its stress, method and material state are attached. It does not certify how long a printed housing, fixture or tool will hold its shape in service. Tg, Vicat and a no-load heat-stability claim answer different questions. ISO [details] · ASTM International [details]

The example below keeps one commercial grade’s as-printed and annealed HDT results separate at two test stresses. It illustrates how to read a sheet, rather than ranking PLA, PETG, ASA and PC from unrelated thermal numbers. ISO [details] · Polymaker [details]

One grade, two stresses, two thermal states

Polymaker labels these HT-PLA results as printed or annealed. The sheet does not identify HDT-specific orientation, geometry, heating rate, conditioning or specimen count. Unknowns remain visible in each test record; the listed values are supplier typical results, not component design limits. Polymaker [details] · ISO [details]

Engineering interpretation. Read the stresses as separate tests. The annealed result at the lower stress cannot replace the higher-stress result when screening a loaded feature. Neither is a safe operating temperature for an arbitrary geometry. Polymaker [details] · Polymaker [details] · ISO [details]

Tg, HDT and Vicat measure different responses

Engineering interpretation. A DSC Tg comes from a heat-capacity change. A DMA Tg is assigned from a change in viscoelastic response; the technique and its frequency matter. Ask which definition was used, and retain the scan rate, thermal history and conditioning when reported. Do not treat a Tg entry as a load-bearing endurance result. ASTM International [details] · ASTM International [details]

Engineering interpretation. HDT observes deflection while a loaded specimen is heated. ISO 75-2 distinguishes a 0.45 MPa stress from a 1.8 MPa stress. Vicat instead observes needle penetration. A higher Vicat value does not supply the missing HDT value for a housing wall or fixture arm. ISO [details] · ASTM International [details]

Keep ASTM D648 results outside an ISO 75 numerical comparison. The ASTM scope explicitly distinguishes their technical content. Matching the word “HDT” or rounding their stress labels does not make the methods interchangeable. ASTM International [details]

Treat annealing as a manufacturing state to qualify

Independent work by Benwood and colleagues on True Red PolyLite PLA supports a mechanism: the printing and post-print thermal history changed crystallinity and the measured heat-deflection response. That study used its own FDM process and ASTM D648 procedure. Its results and annealing recipes are kept separate from the HT-PLA supplier table. ACS Omega [details]

Engineering interpretation. For an annealed candidate, qualify the complete sequence: printing, the actual part-temperature history, support during heating, cooling and final inspection. Check critical holes, locating surfaces and assembled fit after treatment. If the annealing schedule behind a published result is unknown, request it before treating a recommendation as a reproduced test condition. Polymaker [details] · ACS Omega [details]

Use the service load to choose the next measurement

Engineering interpretation. For a hot locating fixture, measure drift at the locating surface while it carries the required force. For an electronics housing, inspect loaded standoffs, latches and fasteners after the required thermal hold and cycles. For a tool touching a hot workpiece briefly, reproduce the contact duration and cooling cycle. These examples define component checks; they are not claims that the illustrated grade has passed them. ISO [details]

Long holds need time-dependent evidence. Fischbach and Weinberg observed continuing creep in a printed Ultrafuse PLA specimen below its glass transition during a room-temperature hold. This does not predict the HT-PLA grade’s life; it shows why being below Tg alone cannot establish dimensional stability under sustained load. Fischbach and Weinberg [details]

Engineering interpretation. If the candidate lacks appropriate creep or retained-property data, test a representative part at its service temperature and environment. Include the load path and attachment points. Define allowable drift and permanent set before the test; do not invent a universal temperature margin below HDT. ISO [details] · Fischbach and Weinberg [details]

What these records establish and what remains unknown

The supplier’s “as printed” thermal labels establish a printed-material basis, but its mechanical coupon drawings do not establish the HDT orientation. General specimen instructions are also not a confirmed HDT recipe. Annealing state is known where labeled; the exact thermal-test annealing schedule remains unknown. Polymaker [details]

A similar source-boundary issue appears in the Prusament PC Blend sheet: HDT is in its typical-material table, outside the separately labeled printed-mechanical table. This article does not assign those thermal values the tensile specimen’s orientation or printing recipe. Ask the supplier for the thermal specimen process before comparing that row with confirmed printed tests. Prusa Polymers a.s. [details]

The comparison still needs HDT specimen geometry, build orientation, heating rate, conditioning and replicate information, plus sustained-load or component evidence for the proposed use. A document access date records consultation, not a fresh test. Polymaker [details] · ISO [details]

Before selecting a hot fixture, housing or tool

Engineering interpretation. Use these as project checks, with acceptance limits chosen for the actual function. A thermal label alone cannot close them. ISO [details]

  1. Record the exact grade and lot where relevant, fabrication process, orientation and full post-processing state. Preserve the measured part-temperature history if annealed.

  2. Check the temperature at critical loaded features, including local heat sources and the required hold or contact cycle.

  3. Verify displacement, permanent set and fit under the actual supports, fasteners and load; repeat the relevant thermal and moisture conditions.

  4. Where endurance matters, obtain or generate time-dependent data over the required conditions. Recheck the geometry or manufacturing route if the evidence cannot support the required margin. ISO [details] · Fischbach and Weinberg [details]

  2. Effect of physical aging on the flexural creep in 3D printed thermoplastic

    Fischbach and Weinberg · Independent experiment · document date: 2023-08-16 · version: arXiv:2302.11240v2 · accessed 2026-10-18
