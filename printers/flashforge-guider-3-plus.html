Problems & troubleshooting

Motion without extrusion: check loading before dismantling the nozzle

The Guider 3 Plus manual separates incomplete filament loading from a nozzle clog. Confirm that material reaches the extruder, then use the printer’s maintenance instructions if the nozzle remains blocked.

Applies to: Guider 3 Plus user manual, chapter 7 Q1–Q4 (English p.22). Its instructions differ from other Guider models.
