Problems & troubleshooting

No extrusion: distinguish a nozzle clog from an extruder jam

Creality separates nozzle/throat blockage, filament trapped inside the extruder and an extruder motor that does not turn. Difficulty both extruding and withdrawing filament directs inspection toward the extruder; the page links separate cleaning and motor-diagnostic procedures.

Applies to: Ender-3 V3 KE toolhead and motor wiring; use the KE service videos rather than SE or CoreXZ V3 disassembly instructions.

Hardware & upgrades

FlowTech hotend alternative

The M3103 kit replaces the Ender-3 V3 KE hotend with a matched heater/thermistor assembly and the FlowTech nozzle system, which permits cold nozzle changes. Follow the kit installation and recalibrate for the installed nozzle and filament.

Applies to: Ender-3 V3 KE-specific M3103 heater/thermistor assembly; SE and CoreXZ V3 kits are different.

Printable modification

FatBurner dual-5015 cooling conversion

The creator’s slimmer mount carries two 5015 blowers with separate ducts, cable routing and optional covers. Before printing, inspect the extruder motor: this mount requires side screw holes, and the listing directs motors without them to a different remix.

Applies to: Ender-3 V3 KE with the specified motor mounting holes and matching fans/fasteners. Not the SE or CoreXZ V3 toolhead.
