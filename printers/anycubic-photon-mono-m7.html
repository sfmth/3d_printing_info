Problems & troubleshooting

Official FPGA update for abnormal exposure switching

Anycubic identifies a specific recovery use for the M7 FPGA firmware: abnormal exposure switching during a print. Use the FPGA download under the M7 support page and its update instructions for that symptom.

Applies to: Photon Mono M7, not M7 Pro/Max. This is a manufacturer recovery update, not modified motion-control firmware.
