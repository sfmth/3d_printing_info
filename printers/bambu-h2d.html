Hardware & upgrades

Calibrate motion with the Vision Encoder accessory

The encoder plate is a calibration accessory used with the toolhead camera, not a build surface. Follow the prescribed calibration conditions and keep the markers clean; the calibration does not replace mechanical inspection when a motion problem appears.

Applies to: The checked product documentation specifies H2D. A functioning toolhead camera is required.

Problems & troubleshooting

Extrusion stops: follow the H2D feed-path diagnosis

The H2D guide checks the hotend and dual-extruder feed path separately when overload or air-printing appears. TPU has a separate diagnostic route because its behavior in the mechanism differs.

Applies to: H2D only; match the installed toolhead and hotend. This documented diagnostic route does not establish failure frequency.

Hardware & upgrades

Convert the toolhead system with the H2C upgrade kit

Bambu documents a substantial Vortek conversion involving the toolhead/X-axis assembly, heatbed, AP board and hotend rack. It requires the model’s complete compatibility pack and device registration; this is a hardware rebuild rather than a firmware-only unlock.

Applies to: H2D compatibility pack only. Follow the H2D guide, including its laser-version differences and current firmware prerequisite.

Problems & troubleshooting

A clumping alert may need both a physical check and a software check

Owners have reported nozzle-clumping alerts on prints without an obvious clump. Check the nozzle and print first, record the exact firmware and retain logs for support. The report is evidence of an observed symptom, not proof that every alert is false or that a current release still has the issue.

Applies to: H2D owner comments in the linked issue. Firmware versions differ between reports; no universal fix is established here.
