Variants & sibling models — and how to tell them apart (spec-contamination risk)Middle model of the Neptune 4 family. Against the base Neptune 4: bigger (320 x 320 x 385 mm), but a lower bed ceiling (100 C rather than 110 C) and much lower acceleration (12000 rather than 20000 mm/s2). Against the Max: smaller and lighter (14.4 kg against 18.1 kg) but with a higher bed ceiling (100 C against 85 C) and higher acceleration (12000 against 8000). Its Z drive is 2 lead screws with 2 tie rods, where the Centauri machines use 3 lead screws

Problems & troubleshooting

Feeding stops: inspect cooling and gearbox before replacing parts

The clogging guide checks filament/profile agreement, heat-break fan operation, gearbox debris and worn PTFE/nozzle parts. It specifies that the heat-break fan should start above 50 °C and requires a new Z offset and leveling after nozzle or PTFE replacement.

Applies to: Neptune 4 Plus; the source explicitly identifies Plus/Max custom nozzles. Do not substitute a Neptune 4/4 Pro nozzle or assume another hotend’s PTFE dimensions.

Hardware & upgrades

FlowTech hotend alternative

The M3108 kit replaces the Neptune 4 Plus / 4 Max hotend with a matched heater/thermistor assembly and the FlowTech nozzle system, which permits cold nozzle changes. Follow the kit installation and recalibrate for the installed nozzle and filament.

Applies to: Original Neptune 4 Plus/Max; select M3108 rather than the shorter Neptune 4/Pro kit. New Plus+/Max+ revisions are not established here.

Firmware & software

OpenNept4une replacement software stack

OpenNept4une replaces the factory software with an updatable Armbian and Klipper stack and supplies printer configurations and calibration tools. Back up the original eMMC image and configuration before following the installation guide.

Applies to: Original Neptune 4 Plus; choose its exact board/model image. New Plus+ and Max+ revisions require separate confirmation; stock update packages are not interchangeable.
