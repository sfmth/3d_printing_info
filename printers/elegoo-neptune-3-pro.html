Problems & troubleshooting

Stopped extrusion: check the feedstock, fan and PTFE path

ELEGOO’s nozzle-clogging procedure starts with filament size and slicer configuration, then checks the heatbreak fan, gearbox and PTFE installation. These checks help separate a nozzle obstruction from heat creep or an upstream feeding fault.

Applies to: Neptune 3 Pro/Plus/Max are named by the source; the Pro uses its own replacement parts and configuration.
