Printable modification

Split vat lid with handle

The creator divides this M5s vat lid into left/right sections, a center connection and handle so it can be printed on a smaller FDM bed. It covers the removed vat during storage; it does not replace the printer’s UV enclosure.

Applies to: Photon Mono M5/M5s vat geometry explicitly named. Check fit before putting a printed cover over liquid resin.

Problems & troubleshooting

Wi-Fi setup: keep the phone on the printer hotspot

The M5s guide explains that the printer hotspot initially has no internet access, which can cause the phone to switch networks. Check the selected hotspot and disable automatic network switching during binding before investigating the router connection.

Applies to: Photon Mono M5s series and the Anycubic app binding workflow; hotspot association and internet Wi-Fi connection are separate stages.
