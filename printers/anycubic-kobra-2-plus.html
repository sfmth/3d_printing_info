Removable Kobra 2 hotend cover

The creator’s replacement cover makes hotend and fan access easier while retaining the stock fan/duct option. It uses the case hardware plus three M3×10 screws; the listing also links a separate custom duct.

Applies to: Creator explicitly lists the Kobra 2 series. Check cover and duct versions together; Neo users additionally need the separate endstop adapter.

Problems & troubleshooting

Module abnormal: verify rebound and nozzle position

The module-error procedure first checks whether the calibration button returns freely. Run Position Calibration and confirm that the nozzle is centered over the module; save the correction and repeat leveling. Wiring checks follow if positioning is correct.

Applies to: Kobra 2 Plus offset-measurement module and its Position Calibration menu. Power down before following the source’s connector-inspection steps.
