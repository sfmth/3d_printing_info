Bambu Lab H2C

Top of the H2 family: dual-nozzle, 350 C hot end, 120 C bed, heated chamber and four cameras as standard, at more than twice the price of the H2S.

Firmware and openness

Closed-source proprietary firmware - not a Klipper or Marlin derivative, and no printer-firmware source is published. The bambulab GitHub organisation carries 11 repositories (Bambu Studio, wxWidgets/boost/ffmpeg forks, the wiki); none is printer firmware.
