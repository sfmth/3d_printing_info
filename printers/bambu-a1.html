Bambu Lab A1

256 mm cube bed-slinger with full-metal linear rails on all three axes, nozzle-touch eddy-current bed probing and optional four-colour AMS lite.

Kinematics & Motion Hardware

Bambu Lab A1 — Kinematics & Motion Hardware
Property and unitPublished value and conditionsSources and dates
Kinematics (bedslinger i3 / CoreXY / other)Cartesian bed-slinger: toolhead moves in X on the gantry beam, heatbed moves in Y, gantry moves in Z. Bambu's own wiki calls it a Cartesian coordinate motion system; Bambu's marketing page describes it as a "Bed Slinger with CoreXY Speed", which is a comparison, not a kinematic claim
  • Bambu Lab Wiki - A1 introduction · 2026-08 · doc
  • Bambu Lab A1 product page · 2026-08 · vendor
X-axis guide type (rods / linear rails / V-wheels; size)Linear rail: "a high-precision linear rail and a beam", toolhead on a linear guide slider (Bambu wiki). Rail size not published
  • Bambu Lab Wiki - A1 introduction · 2026-08 · doc
Y-axis guide typeLinear rail: heatbed mounted on a guide-rail slider, belt-driven in Y
  • Bambu Lab Wiki - A1 introduction · 2026-08 · doc
Z-axis: leadscrew count & type, sync mechanism, anti-backlashDual linear rods plus dual leadscrews with nuts, both leadscrews driven from one stepper through a synchronous belt with a quick tensioner and a tension-detection algorithm
  • Bambu Lab Wiki - A1 introduction · 2026-08 · doc
Belts (type, width, e.g. Gates?)Three adjustable belt tensioners (X, Y, Z); a Belt Tension Monitor watches tension and reports out-of-range belts
  • Bambu Lab Wiki - A1 introduction · 2026-08 · doc
Stepper drivers (exact model e.g. TMC2209; UART/SPI; sensorless homing?)Not published. Bambu's wiki says only that the mainboard "incorporates stepper motor drivers"; no driver part number is given and no teardown source was found that names one

Property and unitPublished value and conditionsSources and dates
Filament runout sensor (type, location)Yes - filament run-out sensor, plus filament odometry and a filament-tangle sensor. The tangle sensor is a Hall sensor reading a magnet in the toolhead filament hub: excessive feed resistance pulls the hub down, moving the magnet toward the sensor

Safety

Bambu Lab A1 — Safety
Property and unitPublished value and conditionsSources and dates
Known safety incidents / recalls / fire reports (with sources)Recalled in the US in cooperation with the CPSC: A1 units sold before 30 January 2024 (serial numbers with 'A' as the sixth digit) could suffer heatbed cable damage leading to a short circuit, electric shock or fire. 19 reports of damaged cables, one with observed sparking, no injuries. Remedy was refund plus $80 voucher, or heatbed+cable replacement by a trained technician plus $120 voucher. Current production ships a Kevlar-reinforced heatbed cable with thicker insulation and extended strain relief
