Problems & troubleshooting

Motion starts but filament does not extrude

First confirm filament reaches the extruder and repeat loading if necessary. If feeding is established, use the manual’s Maintenance clog-clearing route.

Applies to: Guider 3 factory extruder; distinguish loading failure from a blocked nozzle.
