Problems & troubleshooting

BM0111: inspect the toolhead communication path

Creality identifies BM0111 as communication failure between the mainboard and nozzle adapter board. Check the nozzle-board indicator and inspect its cable/connectors for damage or poor contact; the procedure escalates unresolved cases to board/cable diagnosis through support.

Applies to: K2 SE nozzle adapter board and cable. The source inconsistently abbreviates the code as BM111 in its first step; use the article heading BM0111.

Hardware & upgrades

FlowTech hotend alternative

The M3116 kit replaces the Creality K2 SE hotend with a matched heater/thermistor assembly and the FlowTech nozzle system, which permits cold nozzle changes. Follow the kit installation and recalibrate for the installed nozzle and filament.

Applies to: K2 SE-specific M3116. This is separate from the K2/K2 Pro/K2 Plus assemblies.
