Problems & troubleshooting

NEO slicing support does not imply remote printing

The manufacturer includes FUNMAT HT in NEO slicing support but excludes it from remote printing. Use the documented local file workflow rather than troubleshooting an unsupported remote feature.

Applies to: FUNMAT HT family, including the Enhanced model; other INTAMSYS remote-printing requirements do not add this capability.
