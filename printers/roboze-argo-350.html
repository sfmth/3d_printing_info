Problems & troubleshooting

Cross-material extruder contamination

The manufacturer specifies a dedicated material for each extruder to avoid clogging. Check material history before treating poor flow as a firmware problem.

Applies to: ARGO 350 matching extruder configuration; the matrix is not a generic cleaning procedure.

Hardware & upgrades

Match the ARGO extruder to the material

Roboze’s matrix distinguishes the ARGO Tip2/Tip3 configurations and materials rather than treating all extruders as interchangeable.

Applies to: ARGO 350 legacy matrix; confirm the installed revision and material-specific tip with authorized service.
