Problems & troubleshooting

Filament reaches the toolhead but will not extrude

The official diagnostic sequence uses unloading behavior to separate an extruder restriction from a hotend blockage. Check the matching toolhead procedure before removing components.

Applies to: X2D only. X2D also has a separate auxiliary-extruder cleaning guide; its dual-path toolhead differs from P2S.
