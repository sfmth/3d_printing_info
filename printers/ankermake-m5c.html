Problems & troubleshooting

Use extrusion/retraction behavior to locate a jam

Anker’s M5C guide distinguishes filament that cannot move either way from filament that retracts but will not extrude. It then gives separate PTFE/extruder access and nozzle-cleaning procedures, helping avoid unnecessary hotend replacement.

Applies to: AnkerMake M5C; the M5 toolhead and replacement extruder are different. Follow the M5C removal sequence.

Hardware & upgrades

Manufacturer hardened-steel nozzle option

Anker lists a 0.4 mm hardened-steel nozzle for abrasive filled filaments. This changes the wear surface; it does not raise the hotend, bed or material-processing limits of the underlying printer.

Applies to: Manufacturer explicitly lists M5 and M5C for nozzle V7120HA1. Retain the printer’s appropriate hotend profile and material limits.

Printable modification

Clip-on waste box V2

This box collects small filament scraps beside the M5C. Version 2 changes the mast grip, adds a taller wall and extends under the build-plate area; the creator specifies a diagonal print orientation to fit the bed.

Applies to: AnkerMake M5C mast geometry; verify clearance through the complete bed movement after fitting.
