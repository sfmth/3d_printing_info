Variants & sibling models — and how to tell them apart (spec-contamination risk)Base model of the K1 family. The differences that matter are in the hotend: the K1 uses a metal + PTFE-tube hotend with a copper nozzle, while the K1C is all-metal with a steel-tipped nozzle integrated with the heatbreak and adds an air filter and a 1080p AI camera. Creality's own comparison table annotates the K1 hotend "Changing to All-metal Soon", so the hotend on a given unit depends on when it was built - check before buying. The K1 Max is the 300 mm version with 1000 W rated power and Ethernet; the K1 SE has a PA+30%GF hotend and an epoxy plate

Problems & troubleshooting

Idling or clicking extruder: locate the feed obstruction

Start at the rear spool and PTFE inlet, then compare manual feeding with automatic feeding. Creality’s decision tree separates tangled filament, an extruder-front-cover jam, debris on the gears and a hotend blockage before considering motor or nozzle replacement.

Applies to: K1 stock feed path. The source contains legacy nozzle-replacement references; identify the installed hotend before ordering parts.

Hardware & upgrades

CFS multi-material upgrade kit

Creality’s kit changes the toolhead/feed system to support CFS multi-color printing. Check the hotend-identification procedure first and use the kit’s installation, firmware and filament-loading workflow together.

Applies to: K1, K1 Max, K1C and K1 SE; manufacturer excludes K1C 2025 and K1 Max 2025. Earlier K1/K1 Max hotend compatibility must be checked.
