Problems & troubleshooting

A slanted rear bed mesh can indicate frame alignment

The Trident guide describes a mesh that is level across the front but slants across the rear when the Y extrusions are not in plane. Check the supporting surface and frame geometry rather than expecting three-point Z tilt to correct every rear-corner discrepancy.

Applies to: Trident-specific diagnostic pattern; it is not evidence that every uneven mesh has this cause.

Printable modification

Use the matching printed parts for the smooth-pin conversion

Hartk’s pin modification replaces specified M5 shafts with 5 mm smooth pins and matching printed supports. Use the parts for the correct machine revision; the V2-specific Z idlers are not Trident parts.

Applies to: Maintainer explicitly supports V2.4 R2 and Trident; choose the 6 mm or 9 mm belt variant and obtain the accompanying hardware.

Hardware & upgrades

Tap replaces the Z-probe mechanism

Tap uses nozzle contact with the bed and an optical switch. Adoption involves its mechanical assembly, sensor wiring and Klipper configuration, so check bed rigidity and toolhead compatibility before planning the conversion.

Applies to: Voron V2 / Trident with the documented front-mounted extruder, MGN12H rail, rigid bed and compatible power/PCB. Not a V0 modification.

Problems & troubleshooting

After a build or wiring change, recheck the startup sequence

Voron’s commissioning route separates temperature sensing, endstop states, motor motion and homing checks. Use the correct model’s sequence to isolate an incorrect configuration or connection before tuning print quality.

Applies to: Select V1/Trident; its three-point bed system differs from V2’s moving gantry.

Firmware & software

Organize Klipper configuration with Klippain

Klippain provides modular configuration and macros on top of a working Klipper installation. Match the board pins, toolhead and calibration to the actual build instead of replacing printer.cfg with another owner’s complete configuration.

Applies to: The maintainer lists Voron Trident among supported builds. This is a configuration framework, not a universal precompiled firmware image.
