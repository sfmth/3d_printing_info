Problems & troubleshooting

Under-extrusion: locate the restriction before replacing parts

If extrusion becomes thin or stops, first check the material profile, temperature and speed. Bambu’s diagnostic guide then separates a feed-path restriction, extruder blockage and hotend clog so cleaning targets the affected part.

Applies to: A1 and A1 mini. Follow the A1 Series procedure and the matching disassembly guide.

Hardware & upgrades

Add an enclosed AMS through the A1 Series hub

The dedicated hub adds assisted filament feeding for AMS, AMS 2 Pro or AMS HT. The installation also uses a printed bracket specific to the printer. This is a separate connection route from the original AMS lite.

Applies to: A1 / A1 mini firmware 01.07.00.00 or later, A1 Series AMS Hub and matching bracket. Up to four supported AMS units; AMS lite cannot be mixed with them on this series. Select the printed A1 mini bracket, not its sibling’s bracket.
