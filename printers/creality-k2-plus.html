Hardware & upgrades

FlowTech with the K2 Plus heater variant

This replacement changes to FlowTech cold-change nozzles. Select K2 Plus specifically, because its thermistor parameters differ from the K2 and K2 Pro versions.

Applies to: K2 Plus selector option; do not use the K2/K2 Pro heater variant or the K2 SE kit.

Problems & troubleshooting

FR2836: locate resistance between CFS hub and extruder

For this feeding code, inspect spool tangles, deformed spools and the PTFE path between the hub outlet and extruder detector. The official procedure then tests the hub motor and filament detector if the path is clear.

Applies to: K2, K2 Pro, K2 Plus and Creality Hi with CFS; these exact models are listed. This is a code-specific procedure, not a frequency claim.
