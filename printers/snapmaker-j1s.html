Problems & troubleshooting

A nozzle blob needs an adhesion check as well as cleanup

Snapmaker’s shared blob-removal guide explicitly links the J1/J1s hotend procedure. It connects buildup around the nozzle with first-layer adhesion and filament condition, then describes cleanup and replacement paths with care around heater and thermistor wiring.

Applies to: Shared Snapmaker guidance with J1/J1s-specific hotend removal link. Follow the linked model procedure and material conditions rather than applying another module’s disassembly sequence.

Hardware & upgrades

J1/J1s hotend and nozzle-material options

Manufacturer hotend variants change nozzle diameter and wear resistance. The all-metal 0.4 mm hardened-steel version has an interference-fit nozzle rather than an individually replaceable threaded nozzle.

Applies to: J1/J1s only; confirm whether the selected assembly uses a threaded or interference-fit nozzle.
