Hardware & upgrades

FlowTech hotend alternative

The M3106 kit replaces the Ender-3 V3 / V3 Plus hotend with a matched heater/thermistor assembly and the FlowTech nozzle system, which permits cold nozzle changes. Follow the kit installation and recalibrate for the installed nozzle and filament.

Applies to: CoreXZ Ender-3 V3 and V3 Plus, explicitly named by the manufacturer; not the V3 SE or KE hotend.

Problems & troubleshooting

First-layer adhesion or infill scraping: verify the setup

Creality checks plate contamination, filament/profile mismatch and the print-calibration setting before changing Z offset. For infill scraping, its procedure compares the infill pattern with a supplied pre-sliced file to help separate slicing settings from a machine problem.

Applies to: Ender-3 V3 Plus stock leveling workflow; use this exact-model page and treat a live Z-offset change as applying to the current print.
