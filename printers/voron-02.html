Voron 0.2

Open-source 120 mm CoreXY sold only as a kit; the smallest machine in the Voron line. Supplied as a kit — kits by LDO, Formbot, HoneyBadger.

Variants & sibling models — and how to tell them apart (spec-contamination risk)Two things get conflated. First, revision: R1 is the current one, adding a filament-runout sensor, MR85 bearings and fastener changes. Second, kit vendor: the LDO/HoneyBadger S1 kit ships an SKR Pico, E3D Revo, HoneyBadger dual-sided PEI/CF bed and 200 W supply, while the Formbot kit ships a TZ V6 or Dragon hotend and a 150 W supply, so hotend and PSU specs are vendor-specific, not model-specific.
