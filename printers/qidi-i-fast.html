Problems & troubleshooting

Use the legacy i-Fast slicer workflow

QIDI explicitly includes i-Fast in the QIDI Print compatibility list, while separating that slicer from its newer high-speed printers. Confirm the selected printer profile when a transferred job behaves incorrectly.

Applies to: i-Fast; the manufacturer lists QIDI Print 6.5.4 for this legacy series, not the X3-generation workflow.
