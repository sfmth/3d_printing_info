Problems & troubleshooting

Eddy-current probe error: check firmware and gantry alignment

The official guide first checks the sensor drive-current reading and firmware version: from 2.1.19, both readings 14 and 15 are accepted. If that reading is valid, it directs owners to gantry/bed alignment checks instead of immediately replacing the probe.

Applies to: SV08 Max eddy-current probe and its vendor firmware; thresholds are specific to this documented system.
