Hardware & upgrades

FlowTech hotend alternative

The M3110 kit replaces the Sovol SV08 hotend with a matched heater/thermistor assembly and the FlowTech nozzle system, which permits cold nozzle changes. Follow the kit installation and recalibrate for the installed nozzle and filament.

Applies to: Original Sovol SV08-specific M3110; the source does not establish SV08 Max or Zero compatibility.

Problems & troubleshooting

Probe triggered before movement: check sensor and gantry

For this QGL/probing error, Sovol checks proximity-switch response to metal, sensor wiring and gantry alignment. The guide then repeats QGL after correction; an unresponsive switch is referred to support rather than bypassed in configuration.

Applies to: Original SV08 proximity-switch and QGL arrangement; this does not apply to the SV08 Max eddy-probe firmware matrix.

Firmware & software

SV08 mainline Klipper conversion

The maintainer documents replacing the vendor stack with a BTT CB1 image and a standard Klipper/Moonraker/Mainsail installation. The procedure includes eMMC imaging, configuration backup and flashing both the mainboard and toolhead controllers.

Applies to: Original Sovol SV08; this procedure is not verified for SV08 Max. Requires access to the eMMC and separate MCU firmware builds.
