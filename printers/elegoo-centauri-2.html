Problems & troubleshooting

Nozzle blockage: inspect wear, then test extrusion

ELEGOO’s shared guide starts by checking nozzle-tip wear, then uses a controlled extrusion test, cleaning needle and cold-pull route. Follow the illustrated toolhead reassembly and test before returning to a long print.

Applies to: Centauri 2/Combo and Centauri Carbon 2/Combo are explicitly named; use the matching toolhead and temperature instructions.

Hardware & upgrades

Add CANVAS multi-material feeding

The manufacturer offers a CANVAS feeder kit to add automatic multi-spool feeding. Select the mounting/cover configuration for the open-frame Centauri 2 and use its installation and filament workflow.

Applies to: Centauri 2; the listed CC2/C2 kit is separate from the first-generation Centauri Carbon CANVAS kit. The Combo already includes CANVAS.
