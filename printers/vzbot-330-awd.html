Problems & troubleshooting

Driver overheating: verify the external-driver cooling

The Mellow electronics guide requires heatsinks and active cooling for the external TMC5160 drivers. Inspect that cooling arrangement when diagnosing temperature-related driver trouble rather than compensating with higher current or speed settings.

Applies to: Mellow Vz330 kit with external TMC5160 HV drivers. Other controlboard/driver combinations need their own thermal requirements.

Problems & troubleshooting

Random MCU disconnects on a Super8 Pro H723 build

The firmware README identifies random disconnection or shutdown on the STM32H723 Super8 Pro configuration and describes switching the affected TMC5160 sections from hardware to software SPI. Confirm the board/chip and current configuration before applying that specific remedy.

Applies to: Super8 Pro with STM32H723 and the documented driver configuration. This does not diagnose unrelated USB or power problems on other boards.

Applies to: Documented Mellow Fly Super8 with TMC5160 HV for XY and TMC2209 for E/Z. The maintainer also requires the linked Linux-process MCU setup.
