Problems & troubleshooting

Clicking extruder or gaps from partial blockage

Check whether filament reaches the nozzle, inspect the Bowden/PTFE path and follow the manufacturer’s partial-versus-full-clog diagnosis before replacing parts.

Applies to: MINI+ stock hotend/PTFE arrangement; Revo-converted machines have a different hotend assembly.

Printable modification

E3D Revo Micro printable mount

E3D’s creator files provide the mounting part needed before disassembling the stock hotend for its Revo Micro conversion.

Applies to: MINI+ Revo Micro conversion, with the matching fan arrangement and wiring hardware; not a standalone stock-hotend upgrade.

Hardware & upgrades

Revo Micro hotend upgrade

The manufacturer documents a MINI+ conversion using a printed mount, replacement heater/thermistor connections and renewed first-layer calibration.

Applies to: MINI+ E3D kit; follow the fan-voltage regulator and cable-clearance requirements. E3D states this conversion needs no replacement firmware.

Firmware & software

Upstream Klipper configuration for MINI+

Klipper publishes the MINI+ Buddy-board pin/configuration file. This conversion uses an external host and browser interface; the stock LCD remains on its bootloader screen.

Applies to: MINI+ STM32F407 Buddy hardware; first flashing requires breaking the board appendix and using its revision-specific DFU procedure.
