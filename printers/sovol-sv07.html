Printable modification

5015 replacement part-fan duct

The creator’s front-mounted duct adapts a 24 V 5015 blower while retaining clearance for the rear auxiliary fan. The listing specifies the fasteners and a connector adapter for the new fan.

Applies to: SV07 and SV07 Plus are explicitly listed. Requires four M3×20 screws, two nuts and compatible fan wiring; do not infer SV06 Plus fit from the tentative suggestion.

Problems & troubleshooting

Screen disappears after a host-system update

The recovery maintainer distinguishes a missing screen with working Wi-Fi from a deeper boot failure. For the network-reachable case, the guide describes restoring Sovol’s stock update package from USB; an unreachable host needs a different recovery route.

Applies to: SV07 original Sovol/MKS host setup; preserve its printer.cfg because SV07 Plus geometry/configuration differs. This is stock-system recovery, not a replacement operating system.
