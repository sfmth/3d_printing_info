Printing system

Extrusion, heating, leveling and material handling.

Problems & troubleshooting

Idling or clicking extruder: locate the feed obstruction

Start at the rear spool and PTFE inlet, then compare manual feeding with automatic feeding. Creality’s decision tree separates tangled filament, an extruder-front-cover jam, debris on the gears and a hotend blockage before considering motor or nozzle replacement.

Applies to: K1 SE stock feed path. Use the K1 SE removal sequence and identify the installed nozzle before replacement.

Hardware & upgrades

CFS multi-material upgrade kit

Creality’s kit changes the toolhead/feed system to support CFS multi-color printing. Check the hotend-identification procedure first and use the kit’s installation, firmware and filament-loading workflow together.

Applies to: K1, K1 Max, K1C and K1 SE; manufacturer excludes K1C 2025 and K1 Max 2025. Earlier K1/K1 Max hotend compatibility must be checked.
