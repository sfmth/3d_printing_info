Hardware & upgrades

Official Top Cover with controlled circulation and filtration

The optional cover provides exhaust and recirculation modes tied to material profiles. The manufacturer documents automatic heat protection, filter service prompts and software fixes for incorrect fan-mode selection.

Applies to: Snapmaker U1 official Top Cover; check current firmware and Snapmaker Orca compatibility. Its filtration claims do not apply to an unfiltered printed top-hat enclosure.

Problems & troubleshooting

No toolhead detected: distinguish one faulty head from a shared supply fault

The official error-code guide uses the toolhead indicator and one-head-at-a-time checks to distinguish a toolhead failure from a shared power problem. It also documents PCB capacitor damage from incorrect or overtightened cable screws.

Applies to: U1 error 0003-0522-0100-0006. Disconnect power before plugging or unplugging cables; follow the manufacturer’s full diagnostic sequence.
