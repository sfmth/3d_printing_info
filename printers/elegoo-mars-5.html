Problems & troubleshooting

Residue warning at the start of a print

The Mars 5 manual first checks that the tank and build plate are secured and that the machine is untouched during self-check. If the warning persists, it directs cleaning the tank or filtering the resin for small cured fragments.

Applies to: Mars 5 manufacturer manual V1, FAQ item 5, printed page 11. The cited PDF is a mirrored manufacturer document; this procedure is separate from Ultra tilt-release maintenance.
