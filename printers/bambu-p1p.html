Printable modification

Raise the glass top beneath an AMS

Jericho’s riser provides a printed support between a glass top and the AMS. Confirm the surrounding enclosure, clearance and chosen print material for your setup rather than treating a printed riser as temperature-certified hardware.

Applies to: Creator lists P1P and P1S with a glass top. An open, stock P1P needs the corresponding enclosure/top arrangement.

Hardware & upgrades

Convert the open P1P with the official enclosure kit

The kit adds the enclosure and the cooling components specified in Bambu’s installation sequence. The documented firmware accessory flag lets a converted P1P use the corresponding P1S profiles.

Applies to: P1P enclosure conversion. The accessory flag requires P1 firmware 01.05.00.00 or later and Bambu Studio 1.8.1 or later; complete the hardware sequence first.

Printing system

Extrusion, heating, leveling and material handling.

Problems & troubleshooting

Inconsistent extrusion: inspect the gears and feed path

Filament debris and wear in the extruder can contribute to clogs and inconsistent flow. Bambu documents inspection, cleaning and lubrication for the P1 extruder, with reassembly checks before printing again.

Applies to: P1P / P1S. Use the P1 electrical connectors and cleaning procedure; the X1 complete hotend is a different assembly.

Official troubleshooting · Sources & dates
  • P1 extruder cleaning and lubrication ↗ · Checked 2026-10-20
Hardware & upgrades

Use hardened drive gears when moving to abrasive filament

Bambu lists a hardened extruder gear assembly for the P1 series as an alternative to the stock stainless assembly. Pair the drive-path change with a suitable hardened nozzle and the material’s requirements; the gears alone do not make every abrasive filament suitable.

Applies to: P1P / P1S gear assembly. Select the matching P1 hotend components; do not assume the P2S uses this assembly.
