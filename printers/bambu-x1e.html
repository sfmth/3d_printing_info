Problems & troubleshooting

Enterprise Wi-Fi setup and unexpected network priority

The X1E prioritizes Ethernet when wired and wireless links are both connected. Certificate-based enterprise Wi-Fi must be configured after initial setup; the guide explains certificate formats, physical network switches and IP/DNS settings.

Applies to: X1E networking only. Obtain the appropriate enterprise certificates from the network administrator; X1C connection instructions do not cover these controls.
