Variants & sibling models — and how to tell them apart (spec-contamination risk)Base model of a five-way family that is easy to confuse. All of Kobra 2, Kobra 2 Neo and Kobra 2 Pro share the same 220 x 220 x 250 mm build volume, but their speed ceilings differ: Neo 250 mm/s max / 150 typical, Kobra 2 300 / 200, Kobra 2 Pro 500 / 300. The Plus (320 x 320 x 400 mm) and Max (420 x 420 x 500 mm) are bigger but drop the bed ceiling to 90 C where the three small ones reach 110 C. Anycubic's own note: Kobra 2 firmware must be at V3.0.6 or above for the quoted speed

Problems & troubleshooting

Uneven first layer: check bed-wheel contact

For an uneven first layer, Anycubic directs owners to run leveling and check that the Y-axis bed wheels contact their rails. Its FAQ also describes eccentric-nut and printhead-fastener checks when the bed or head wobbles.

Applies to: Original Kobra 2 wheel and printhead assemblies; this is the Kobra 2 FAQ, not a Pro/Plus/Max calibration procedure.
