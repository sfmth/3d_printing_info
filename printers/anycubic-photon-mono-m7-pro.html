Problems & troubleshooting

False “platform not installed” warning

The official sequence reseats the platform, runs Advanced → Equipment Self-test → Force Detection, then checks the strain-gauge connection. If the warning remains, it gives a mechanical-calibration procedure with separate platform-removed and platform-installed steps.

Applies to: Photon Mono M7 Pro only. Use its clean, empty platform state during calibration; the source is not a failure-frequency study.
