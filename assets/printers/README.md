# Printer guide images

`data/printer-images.json` records the exact catalog printer ID, local WebP, original image URL, source page, publisher credit, inspection date, pixel dimensions and SHA-256 digest for each curated image. All 132 catalog models have product pictures checked on 2026-10-20 and visually inspected before acceptance. Sources are manufacturer or project pages, with exact-model specialist dealer listings for some legacy, industrial and kit configurations. Images are resized proportionally to at most 1200 pixels on either side and encoded as WebP; they are not generated product imagery.

Copyright remains with the manufacturers or other image owners. Source links and credits identify provenance; they do not establish an open license or blanket permission to reuse these files. No open reuse license is asserted for these manufacturer images. The Voron 0.2 photograph is published by Voron Design; the Voron 2.4 image comes from Formbot's explicitly named R2 Ultra kit page. These are example builds, so neither image establishes the components supplied by another kit vendor. In particular, the Formbot image does not depict or identify an LDO kit.

Product images may show filament spools, sample prints, enclosures, material systems or other accessories. Descriptive alt text identifies important configurations; a pictured accessory is not a claim that every catalog configuration includes it. Manufacturer promotional badges remain part of their original images. No image is reused for a sibling model just because its enclosure or printer family looks similar. The XL pictures depict the current XL+ with the stated tool count, and the CORE One+ picture comes from the current Gen 2 listing. The gold MK3S+ is the 10th Anniversary Edition. LDO Positron, LDO Trident R2, Mellow VzBoT and Rat Rig V-Core 4 pictures show the configurations named in their source listings; kit contents and user builds can vary. The original Revo 14K and V-Core 4 pictures are not substituted with Revo 16K or V-Core 4.1 images.

Blank white canvas is trimmed where useful. The Ender-3 V4 and QIDI X-Plus 3 pictures are cropped from their official product scenes while retaining the complete printer. The Factor 4 front view is extracted, with its transparency mask, from page 1 of UltiMaker’s original Factor 4 datasheet; the PDF is recorded as both the source and original asset URL. The F270 uses Ricoh’s exact-model product graphic, which contains a small whole-printer photograph; it has less detail than the newer product images. Original source resolution is preserved when it is below the size limit.

Dealer provenance includes MatterHackers, Fabreeko, Tosey Innovation, 3DJake, EXBuild, BFI3D, Ricoh and Top 3D Shop. Their names remain in credits where their pages supplied an image. Rat Rig’s [dealer list](https://ratrig.com/pages/dealers) identifies Tosey Innovation, [EXBuild](https://www.exbuild.com/about) identifies its Markforged partnership, and [3DGence’s partner list](https://3dgence.com/america/milam/) identifies BFI Innovation. Such relationships identify the source; they do not grant an image reuse license.

The six SVG architecture diagrams generated by `scripts/printer_design.py` are original site illustrations. They are schematic diagrams, not product images, photographs or depictions of a particular printer model. Their provenance is separate from the manufacturer-image manifest.

To update an image, reopen the official source, confirm the exact model and pictured configuration, inspect the downloaded candidate, optimize it without changing the printer's appearance, and update all manifest metadata and the digest together. Preserve a source URL that identifies the model, rather than only a CDN link or a search-result URL. Do not infer permission from a printer's open-source hardware license.
