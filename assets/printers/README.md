# Printer guide images

`data/printer-images.json` records the exact catalog printer ID, local WebP, original image URL, official source page, publisher credit, inspection date, pixel dimensions and SHA-256 digest for each curated image. The 69 product images were checked on 2026-10-20 against manufacturer or project pages and visually inspected before acceptance. Images are resized proportionally to at most 1200 pixels on either side and encoded as WebP; they are not generated product imagery.

Copyright remains with the manufacturers or other image owners. Source links and credits identify provenance; they do not establish an open license or blanket permission to reuse these files. No open reuse license is asserted for these manufacturer images. The Voron 0.2 photograph is published by Voron Design; the Voron 2.4 image comes from Formbot's explicitly named R2 Ultra kit page. These are example builds, so neither image establishes the components supplied by another kit vendor. In particular, the Formbot image does not depict or identify an LDO kit.

Product images may show filament spools, sample prints, enclosures, material systems or other accessories. Descriptive alt text identifies important configurations; a pictured accessory is not a claim that every catalog configuration includes it. Manufacturer promotional badges remain part of their original images. No image is reused for a sibling model just because its enclosure or printer family looks similar. Models without a verified image use a clearly labelled architecture diagram instead.

The six SVG architecture diagrams generated by `scripts/printer_design.py` are original site illustrations. They are schematic diagrams, not product images, photographs or depictions of a particular printer model. Their provenance is separate from the manufacturer-image manifest.

To update an image, reopen the official source, confirm the exact model and pictured configuration, inspect the downloaded candidate, optimize it without changing the printer's appearance, and update all manifest metadata and the digest together. Preserve a source URL that identifies the model, rather than only a CDN link or a search-result URL. Do not infer permission from a printer's open-source hardware license.
