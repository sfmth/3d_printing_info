# Printed examples and image attribution

Material reports from the linked Printables listings were checked on 2026-10-17. Attribution and reuse handling were reviewed on 2026-10-29; this does not claim that every current listing panel was independently revalidated. The reports are not independent material tests or certification. Never infer an exact grade from a related polymer or a recommendation. Notes identify the relevant component when an image contains several materials.

The manifest now references **29 local image files, representing 28 distinct source-image identities**; two separate material examples use the same drone photograph. `data/print-examples.json` preserves the source-image URL, listing URL, uploader display name, selected licence and link, material evidence, dimensions and SHA-256 for each retained image. Images are optional. Model-only examples retain their material evidence and direct links without assigning an unverified image. PVA is explicitly a sacrificial-support example; its finished model is a separate material.

The legacy `creator` field records the **listing uploader**, as made explicit by `creator_role`. It is not evidence that this person is the photographer or sole model author. Captions and cards identify the uploader; `photo_author` remains null where the photographer is not independently identified. Separately sourced `model_credits` preserve known original-design and remix credits. For the Mouse Figure, Q's listing credits Whitney Richmond for the original design; the credit source remains linked. Additional supplied copyright, attribution or warranty notices belong in `credit_notices` and must remain visible with the image.

## Basis and individual conditions

[Printables Terms of Service](https://www.prusa3d.com/page/terms-of-service-of-printables-com_231249/), effective 2026-10-15, include related image files within Printing Documents (§2.1) and offer those documents under the uploader's selected licence (§2.3). This is a licence offered by the uploader, not a blanket grant from Prusa. The uploader must have the necessary rights (§2.4); the selected licence's conditions continue to apply (§2.6). A listing licence does not clear unrelated third-party rights.

Downloaded source-hosted image and thumbnail bytes are preserved without modification, including their metadata. This site does not crop, recompress or strip metadata from retained images. Printables may already have generated a thumbnail before it was downloaded. All image placements show the complete downloaded image with `object-fit: contain`; the ND image also has a dedicated no-crop class. Display sizing does not change the file's bytes. Captions, directory/related cards and example disclosures retain the individual licence, uploader, source image, known model credits and an unchanged-image notice.

The **13 NC**, **11 SA** and **1 ND** image files retain their respective conditions. NC images are included for the site's current noncommercial reference use, without advertising or affiliate monetization. A change to that use requires a new review; free access alone does not settle whether a use is noncommercial. SA images retain their own licence, and any future qualifying adaptation must follow its ShareAlike conditions. The ND image is reproduced in full without sharing an adaptation. See the [NC legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode.en), [ND legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/legalcode.en) and [Creative Commons FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/).

These individual image terms are not replaced by the repository's GPL licence. The surrounding compilation does not convert an NC, SA or ND image into a GPL work. Retain all supplied attribution and notices, and link the licence with each image; the [CC BY legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) describes the attribution requirements. The PC/ABS GPL model remains linked without reproducing its image.

## Link-only decision

The Octonauts Fish Biscuit remains linked as an uploader-reported matte-PLA example. Its selected CC BY licence does not establish permission for the underlying franchise design, so `assets/prints/pla-matte.jpg` and its photo metadata were removed. A replacement image was not adopted because its current source-image/licence relationship could not be verified. This is a rights-verification gap, not a finding that the uploader lacks permission. The licence and source remain with the model link.

Current individual listing panels were access-limited during this review, including PA12-CF and PET-CF. Their retained licence records and material-check dates remain 2026-10-17. Do not silently promote that date to a fresh panel verification or infer an unidentified photographer's name.
